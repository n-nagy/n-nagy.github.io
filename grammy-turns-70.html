02

How the weekend flows

Arrive relaxed, one big celebration dinner at the house on Saturday, a lake day Sunday, and an easy departure Monday. Come Friday night or Saturday morning — whatever's easier for your carload.

Friday Sept 4 · arrival Roll in easy
AFTERNOON / EVENING

Check in & settle

Early birds arrive, claim rooms, stock the fridge. Anyone who'd rather leave in the morning can — Saturday's the first "together" day.

NIGHT

Low-key dinner

Order in or grill something simple. Kids to bed, adults by the firepit.

Saturday Sept 5 · Grammy's day 🎂 The main event
MORNING

Everyone's here

Late arrivals roll in. Coffee, pool, playground — the house does the entertaining.

ALL DAY

Day at the house

Pool and swingset for the kids, no agenda for the grown-ups. This is why we picked a place that fills its own day.

EVENING · THE CELEBRATION

Hibachi chef comes to us

A private chef cooks dinner at the house — dinner and a show, no one leaves, no one cooks, no one cleans. Cake and toasts for Grammy to close the night.

Sunday Sept 6 · lake day 🌊 On the water
MORNING

Hit Raystown Lake

Rent a pontoon from Seven Points Marina and bring the jet ski. Swimming coves, tubing, cruising. Note: Sept 6 is the last in-season day — boats run 8:30am–5pm.

ALTERNATIVE · GENTLER PACE

Proud Mary showboat cruise

A calmer option that suits the toddlers and Grammy: a narrated cruise on the lake. Sunday Sept 6 is the final weekend they run.

NIGHT

Easy dinner out or pizza in

After a day on the water, keep it simple — a local spot or pizza back at the house.

Monday Sept 7 · departure Head home
MORNING

Pack up & goodbyes

Slow breakfast, load the cars, and everyone rolls out on the Labor Day holiday with a full weekend behind them.

The fine print, handled

🍽️

Saturday dinner

Book the in-home hibachi chef early — Labor Day weekend fills up. Confirm headcount (14) and any kid-friendly options.

🚤

Boat day

Reserve the pontoon in advance with a deposit. Pontoons can't pass mile marker 15. Jet ski launches separately.

📅

Book the house now

All three are available for Sept 4–7, but big Labor Day houses go fast. Starlit has free cancellation before Aug 21 — lock it while we decide.

🏊

Confirm the pool

Message the Starlit host to confirm the heated pool is operating Labor Day weekend before booking — it's the whole reason it wins.

🐶

No dogs

This trip is dog-free, which keeps every house option open and simplifies the boat rental.

🧳

Arrival flex

Come Friday night or Saturday morning — Saturday is the first full day everyone's together, so no pressure to travel Friday.
