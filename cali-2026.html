Activity guide

Option A — Thursday
Big Basin & Wine
  • Big Basin Redwoods SPAncient old-growth. Sempervirens Falls loop ~3.5mi, easy-moderate. Check trail status — partially restored after 2020 CZU fire.
  • Big Basin VineyardsSanta Cruz Mountains AVA. Pinot Noir and Chardonnay. Tasting room in the hills above Santa Cruz.
  • West Cliff DriveScenic coastal walk — Lighthouse, surfers, seals.
  • Tacos MorenoBest cheap tacos in Santa Cruz. Cash only.
Hike in the morning, wine in the afternoon. Relaxed pace — this is a good day, not a long day.
Options B & C — Friday
Big Sur
  • Bixby Creek BridgeIconic arch bridge on HWY 1 heading south. First major stop.
  • Pfeiffer BeachPurple sand, sea stacks. $12 day use fee. Narrow unmarked turnoff before Pfeiffer Big Sur SP.
  • McWay Falls80ft waterfall onto beach. Short flat trail at Julia Pfeiffer Burns SP.
  • NepentheClifftop lunch, ocean views. Good turnaround point before heading back to Carmel.
Leave Monterey by 8am. Welcome party isn't until 8pm — you have the full day, but factor in the drive back, check-in, and getting ready.
Option B — Thursday
Monterey & Wine
  • 17-Mile DriveScenic toll road through Pebble Beach, Lone Cypress, Bird Rock. ~$12/car.
  • Bernardus WineryTop Carmel Valley producer. Pinot & Chardonnay.
  • Joyce VineyardsSmaller, more intimate. Great Pinot Noir.
  • Carmel VillageWalk Ocean Ave, galleries, coffee at Carmel Bakery.
  • Monterey AquariumWorth it if you haven't been — kelp forest and sea otters are excellent.
Do 17-Mile Drive in the morning, Carmel Valley wine in the afternoon. Wedding is at Folktale Winery Saturday so you'll be at a vineyard either way.

Booking order

  1. Text Jeff & Luciana — booked in Carmel yet? If not, pitch the Fireplace Inn two-room split. First domino.
  2. Book Carmel hotel — whichever scenario plays out, book this week. Saturday Sept 12 goes fast.
  3. Decide itinerary A, B, or C — north vs south vs balanced. Drives outbound flight and remaining hotels.
  4. Flights — American 8:20am Wed + United 8:50am Mon for A or B. United 9:25am Thu + TBD Tue for C.
  5. Monterey hotel (B or C) — Hotel Abrego or Casa Munras. Book direct. Abrego promo expires mid-May.
  6. Santa Cruz hotel (A or C) — Pacific Blue Inn or Hyatt Place. Lower urgency.
  7. Half Moon Bay hotel (A or B) — Oceano or Half Moon Bay Lodge. Book direct.

Key caveats

  • Welcome party is 8pm Friday — festive cocktail attire. Plenty of time for a full day before getting ready.
  • Wedding is 4pm Saturday at Folktale Winery — colorful black tie. Shuttles confirmed from Carmel Mission Inn; Fireplace Inn guests need Uber/Lyft.
  • Big Basin trail status — check current conditions before committing. Partially restored after 2020 CZU fire.
  • Big Sur timing (B & C) — leave Monterey by 8am Friday. You have until ~5pm to get back, check in, and get ready for 8pm.
  • HWY 1 north on Sunday (Options A & B) — Carmel to Half Moon Bay is the iconic coastal stretch. One of the best drives in the country.
  • Rental car — book early, prices rise closer to the date. Non-negotiable for this trip.
  • All hotel rates except confirmed Carmel prices are historical estimates — verify before booking.
