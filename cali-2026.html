Option A — North Focus

Wed Sept 9 – Mon Sept 14 · 4 days PTO each · American 8:20am PHL→SFO out, United 8:50am SFO→PHL back. Prioritizes Santa Cruz beach, Big Basin redwoods, Hallcrest Vineyards, and the scenic HWY 1 drive to Half Moon Bay. Welcome party Friday 8pm in Carmel. Wedding Saturday 4pm at Folktale Winery & Vineyards.

Why Option A: Conscious decision to prioritize the north — Santa Cruz vibe, ancient redwoods, mountain wine country, and the iconic HWY 1 coastal drive. Friday includes a 17-Mile Drive day trip through the Monterey Peninsula on the way to Carmel. Bixby Bridge, McWay Falls — the Big Sur sights — sit further south, past Carmel, so they're out of reach for a day trip starting from Santa Cruz. In other trip options we'd only be spending ~2 hours there anyway, so it's not a huge loss either way.

Activity guide

Thursday — Big Basin
Big Basin Redwoods
  • Big Basin Redwoods SPSempervirens Falls loop ~3.5mi, easy-moderate. Ancient old-growth. Check trail status before going — partially restored after 2020 CZU fire.
  • Berry Creek Falls TrailMore ambitious option — 10mi loop, passes multiple waterfalls. Full day hike if you want it.
  • ParkingReserve day-use parking online in advance at parks.ca.gov — fills up fast on weekdays.
Start early, ideally by 9am. Cooler in the morning and you'll want time for the winery in the afternoon. Backup if Sempervirens Falls is still closed: Henry Cowell Redwoods SP in Felton — old-growth redwoods, Redwood Grove Loop Trail (~0.8mi easy) or the longer Eagle Creek/Pipeline loop. Right on the same route, no detour needed.
Thursday — Wine
Hallcrest Vineyards
  • Hallcrest Vineyards379 Felton Empire Rd, Felton CA. Open Thu–Mon 12–5pm. First certified organic winery in the US. Sit on the deck with a bottle.
  • Wild Roots MarketDown the road from Hallcrest — pick up picnic supplies before the tasting. Cheese, bread, charcuterie.
  • Felton townCute mountain town 5min away. Worth a quick wander after the tasting.
Hallcrest is right on the route back down from Big Basin — stop in Felton on the way back to Santa Cruz. No backtracking needed.
Friday — Pre-Wedding
Carmel Village
  • Carmel BeachWhite sand, turquoise water, dog-friendly. One of the best beaches in California. Worth a morning walk.
  • Ocean AvenueMain drag — galleries, boutiques, coffee. Carmel Bakery for breakfast.
  • 17-Mile DriveMonterey Peninsula day trip on the way south to Carmel — Pebble Beach, Lone Cypress, Bird Rock. ~$12/car toll. A different stretch of coast than the Big Sur sights, which sit further south past Carmel.
  • Carmel MissionBeautiful historic mission a few blocks from the inn. Quick stop.
Welcome party is 8pm — you have the whole day. Keep Friday relaxed so you arrive at the wedding weekend feeling good, not tired.

Santa Cruz — extra ideas for free time

Not built into the sequencing above — just good options if a window opens up.

Coastal & Wharf
  • Natural Bridges State Beach — Iconic rock arch. Good for sunset given a late afternoon arrival.
  • West Cliff Drive — Paved coastal path with ocean views, cliffs, and surfers at Steamer Lane.
  • Santa Cruz Wharf — Fresh seafood at The Crow's Nest, sea lions lounging under the pier.
  • The Boardwalk — Rides closed weekdays after Labor Day, but grounds, beach, and the Casino Arcade stay open. Corn dogs, churros, vintage games, no crowds.
  • Westside / Ingalls St — Converted warehouse district with craft breweries, wine tasting rooms, and farm-to-table dinner options.
Capitola & Mystery Spot
  • Capitola Village — Colorful seaside town a bit east. Pleasure Pizza, sit on the sea wall, browse boutiques. Good detour on the way back from the mountains.
  • The Mystery Spot — Quirky gravity-defying tourist spot tucked in the redwoods near Felton. ~45min guided tour, optical illusions. Fun if there's a gap, not essential.
Thursday is already full with Big Basin, Felton, and Hallcrest — treat these as options only if the day moves faster than expected, not things to force in.
