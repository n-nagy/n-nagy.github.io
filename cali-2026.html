The core tradeoff: Option 1 is the budget/PTO-efficient choice — solid trip, one main exploring day, Big Basin is a bonus if you feel up to it Sunday. Options 2 and 3 both add a 4th PTO day but spend it differently: Option 2 front-loads it with more pre-wedding time in Monterey, Option 3 back-loads it with a dedicated Big Basin day. If Shannon's PTO is tight, Option 1 is the right call. If you can swing 4 days, Option 2 is the better trip — two real days before the wedding feels more relaxed than one.

Booking order (all options)

  • 1. Carmel Saturday hotel immediately — Hofsas House, Tally Ho Inn, or Cypress Inn. Don't wait on this.
  • 2. Decide Option 1 vs 2 vs 3 — drives the outbound flight date and number of Monterey nights.
  • 3. Flights — lock in once option is chosen.
  • 4. Remaining hotels — Monterey and final night can wait a few weeks. Book direct for best rates.

Key caveats

  • Confirm whether there's a welcome event Friday night — affects how much of Big Sur you can do that day
  • Ask wedding couple about a Carmel room block before booking independently
  • Big Basin is optional Sunday in Options 1 and 2 — easy to skip if post-wedding recovery takes priority
  • Half Moon Bay is a legit alternative to Santa Cruz for the final night — closer to SFO, quieter, slightly more upscale
  • All hotel rates are historical estimates — confirm live pricing before booking
  • Car rental: ~$60–80/day + gas. Non-negotiable for this trip.
